Наша заявка — что нести на преколл

Key insight

“AI won't replace marketers. It will replace repetitive marketing operations.”

Proof / context

“We trained AI on thousands of historical marketing decisions across enterprise ad accounts, replacing manual reporting and analysis. Teams now manage over $500M in annual ad spend while cutting routine operational work by up to 90%.”

Actionable takeaway

“Start every week by eliminating one repetitive task instead of adding one new responsibility. If a process happens every week, teach it to AI instead of repeating it yourself.”

Эти три блока идеально садятся на его схему выпуска: Reality Check → Proof Stack → одна привычка на неделю. Так и предложить — как готовый костяк записи, а острый угол пусть выберет сам.
